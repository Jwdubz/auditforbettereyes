What I found

01

The homepage is slow to show its main content.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 3.0 seconds and its largest part, the looping background video at the top, at 7.6 seconds. The page is about 2.2 MB. The test warned that the page was too slow to finish cleanly, so treat the numbers as a rough measure from one run.

02

The homepage says “Schedule a comprehensive consultation” and then gives only a phone number.

After that line the homepage shows the number 702-819-3937 and no button or form (the footer has a small Contact Us link). The appointment-request form is on the Contact page and on four lens and technology pages. The Cataract Surgery, eyelid surgery and pricing pages have the number but no form. The form says “a member of our staff will reach out to you shortly”, with no time given, and I found no online scheduling calendar. I didn't submit the form.

03

Test pages are live and listed for Google.

Your sitemap lists pages called “404-2”, “Test” and “Hello world!”. I opened the Test and Hello world pages and both load. The llms.txt file you publish lists them too. An empty link to startertemplatecloud.com, a site-template provider, also sits at the end of the first paragraph on the homepage.

04

Your business markup is thin.

The homepage markup describes an organization with a name, website, image and social links. It has no phone, address, opening hours or medical-practice type. Your address and phone are on the page, so search tools can still find them in the text.

05

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

Seven button labels are hard to read against their backgrounds, and one empty link has no name.

What's working

Every one of the 9 pages I checked has a page title, a page description and one clear headline. Every page has your phone number as a tap-to-call link and your address in the header. The Contact page has a form with appointment-time choices, a Google map and your clinic details, and there are pricing and financing pages. For Dr. Pizio's name you were in the answer.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether your contact form messages reach you, and how quickly staff reply. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks the WordPress admin area and a form-upload folder. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    You publish an llms.txt file

    /llms.txt exists, generated by your SEO plugin, with a short summary of the site and five pages and one post. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read, and most sites don't have one.

  • Gap
    Business markup is generic

    The homepage markup is typed as an organization with name, website, image and social links. There is no phone, address, hours or medical-practice type.

  • Gap
    Test pages in the sitemap and llms.txt

    Both list “Test” and “Hello world!”, and the sitemap also lists “404-2”. The Test and Hello world pages return 200.

  • Found
    Phone and address match and are easy to find

    The header on every page shows 702-819-3937 and 9560 W. Skye Canyon Park Dr., Ste 180, Las Vegas, NV 89166, as does the Contact page. The phone is a tap-to-call link and the address links to Google Maps.

  • Note
    No online scheduling found

    I found a request form but no booking calendar on the homepage or the Contact page.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
